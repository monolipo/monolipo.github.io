Bright-star catalog attribution
===============================

The file bright_stars_catalog.csv is a 1,000-row filtered subset derived from
HYG Database v4.1 by David Nash / Astronexus:
https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database

Selection used by CSOTG:
- apparent visual magnitude <= 5.0
- declination < +40 degrees
- brightest 1,000 entries after sorting by V magnitude

The filtered catalog retains J2000 RA/DEC and apparent V magnitude fields.
