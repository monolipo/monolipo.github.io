Interactive optical simulator

OptiBench 2D is a browser-based, non-sequential geometric ray-tracing simulator for optical benches, telescopes and spectrographs. It includes spherical, parabolic and hyperbolic mirrors with an optional transmissive central hole for Cassegrain-like configurations.

OptiBench 2D

Open the simulator in a full browser page. No installation or external library is required.
